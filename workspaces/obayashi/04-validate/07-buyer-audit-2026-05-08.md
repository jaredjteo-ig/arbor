## What didn't pass scrutiny

### P1 (blocks Obayashi). HTTPS

Site is http://136.110.51.61. Obayashi's IT will block on day one.
Xero refuses non-localhost HTTP redirect URIs (already noted in
the Xero workspace session notes). **Get a domain + Let's Encrypt
before any enterprise pilot.**

### P1. Registration is invitation-only

Landing page CTA is "Login". Signup is closed. As a true new
customer I cannot self-onboard. For a sales-led motion this is
fine — but the marketing site should make that explicit and offer
a "Book demo / request access" path, not just a login button.

### P2. Work-Pass-Expiring filter is empty in the demo

The filter wires correctly (chip activates, "Clear filter" link
appears, table re-renders with empty state). But none of the 4
foreign workers in the seed data (Tanaka / Sato / Ahmad / Nguyen)
have a pass expiring within the window. The marquee SG
differentiator should LIGHT UP for a buyer. Seed at least one
employee with `work_pass_expiry` in the next 30-60 days.

### P2. WICA Insurance shows $0 with no tooltip

For a SG citizen at $5K in services, WICA isn't statutorily
mandatory (above $2,600 / non-manual). The $0 is technically
correct but a buyer with a manual workforce (Obayashi:
construction) will read $0 as a gap. Add a tooltip:
"WICA mandatory for manual workers and non-manual <$2,600/mo —
your case doesn't trigger the threshold."

### P3. Compliance page scroll behaviour

Page didn't respond to `window.scrollTo` from a Playwright run —
likely an inner-scroll container. Worth checking on a real
1080p laptop monitor that the full checklist is reachable
without trapping the user.

### P3. No pricing or trust signals on the marketing surface

Landing page has no pricing, no compliance badges (ISO/SOC),
no logo wall, no security page. Obayashi procurement will ask
on day one. Even a single "PDPA-compliant, Singapore-hosted,
audited" line above the fold would help.
