## Executive verdict

Gates 2-4 ship a **real** end-to-end story: writes flow, the lifecycle dashboard moves when you mutate it (Give Kudos went 6→7 recognitions_30d in real time, training records went 3→4 the moment I POSTed one), and the data surfaces are coherent across most modules. **But the lifecycle hub — the page Jennifer lands on first — undersells four of its eight stages.** Stage detail blurbs still say things like "Recognition module ships in P2-RC; until then 'kudos 30d' will read 0" even though kudos are live. Quick-action deep-links don't point to the new modules. The L&D card's only link is to a SkillsFuture catalogue endpoint that returns "Tool not found in any MCP server". The activity feed's 20 rows include 9 duplicates of the same onboarding step. And the retention model produces empty drivers for 26 of 28 employees. Jennifer would believe the underlying engine; she would NOT believe the hero surface.

**Single highest-impact fix**: Re-author the four stale stage-detail blurbs (lnd / reward / progression / retain) and add deep-links to /training/records, /recognition, /goals, /exit-interviews. ~2h work, 80% of the credibility uplift.

**Does the lifecycle narrative cohere now?** **Yes-but**. The data plumbing works end-to-end and the eight stages all have live KPIs (no `data_missing:true` anywhere). What breaks coherence is the _presentation layer_ — stale "coming soon" copy, missing deep-links, duplicated activity rows, a broken catalogue page, a retention scorer with no signal on most rows, and one structural contradiction (Retain card shows 0 exits while the Exit-Interviews module shows 2 completed surveys, because S8 only counts EmploymentEvent records and the seed never created the matching exit events). Fix the surface and the story becomes confidently sellable.

## Cross-cutting issues

1. **Stage panels are out of date with the rest of the platform.** This is not a one-line copy fix — it's the _primary visible deliverable_ of "Cox 8-stage lifecycle". The blurbs are what convince a buyer the platform is mature. Currently they advertise immaturity. (HIGH)
2. **Activity feed dedup is missing.** A 14-day cross-stage timeline that repeats one event 7x looks broken. (BLOCKER)
3. **Quick-action deep-link map is incomplete.** Cox stages 5/6/7/8 don't link to their owning modules. (HIGH)
4. **Retention model has no signal on most rows.** A "scored employee table" that scores 50/100 with empty drivers for 92% of the workforce is not a retention model; it's a uniform watchlist. (HIGH)
5. **No exit EmploymentEvent rows** — Retain card claims 0 exits while exit interviews exist. Internal contradiction. (HIGH)
6. **SkillsFuture catalogue silently broken** — empty UI, no error surfaced, only deep-link from S5. (BLOCKER for L&D narrative)

No 5xx errors observed during the walkthrough. No `Unknown` buckets that shouldn't be there (only `gender unknown=1` and the collapsed `<5` rows in pay-equity, both correct). I did not see any reappearance of the round-12 stale-pending state.

## Bottom line for Jennifer Liu

The **engine** is real — write it, see the dashboard move; submit a kudos, watch the lifecycle KPI climb; trigger an exit interview, see the theme tally. The **engine is sellable**. What is currently un-sellable is the lifecycle hero surface itself: stale "coming soon" copy on shipped modules, broken first deep-link from the L&D card, an activity feed that triplicates events, a retention scorer that produces "we don't know" for 26 of 28 people, and a numerical contradiction between Retain (0 exits) and the Exit-Interview module (2 completed surveys). Six fixes — none architectural, all copy/seed/dedup — flip this from "interesting prototype" to "production-grade workforce platform". Without those fixes, Jennifer will pay attention but not pay money.
