# Company Policy Upload — Stakeholder Decisions (2026-04-01)

## Decision Points

1. **Statutory floor enforcement**: Warn (not block) when company policy is below statutory minimums
2. **Employee acknowledgment**: Required — integrate into onboarding flow
3. **Advisory integration**: Searchable by advisory engine from day 1
4. **File upload**: Supported in v1 (PDF/DOCX + manual text entry)
5. **Version history**: Supported from the start
6. **Categories**: 9 predefined categories + option to create custom categories
