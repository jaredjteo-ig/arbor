## Objection Handling

**"How current is the legal data?"**

> "Our knowledge base covers 6 regulatory domains — Employment Act, CPF Act, EFMA, TAFEP/Workplace Fairness Act, WSH Act, and IRAS tax regulations. All updated to 2026 rates. The CPF rate tables are the same ones published by the CPF Board."

**"Can we customise the onboarding modules?"**

> "Completely. You fill out the Excel template with your content, policies, and checklists. When you upload it, everything is created automatically. To edit, update the Excel and re-upload a new version."

**"What about data security? PDPA compliance?"**

> "NRIC and bank account numbers are encrypted at rest using Fernet encryption. All sensitive data access is logged in a PDPA audit trail — who accessed what, when, from which IP. Role-based access control ensures employees only see their own data."

**"Do you integrate with our existing systems?"**

> "We generate CPF e-Submit files, IR8A tax forms, and bank GIRO files in the formats required by CPF Board, IRAS, and Singapore banks. You download and upload — no manual data entry."

**"What if we're using another HRIS?"**

> "The Parallel Run feature lets you upload your existing payslips and compare them against Central's calculations. When the numbers match, you know the switch is safe. We also support CSV bulk employee import."

**"Is this just for Singapore?"**

> "The platform is Singapore-first — CPF, MOM, IRAS, all built in. The onboarding system and HR operations work for any country. The compliance advisory is currently Singapore only, with Thailand expansion planned."
