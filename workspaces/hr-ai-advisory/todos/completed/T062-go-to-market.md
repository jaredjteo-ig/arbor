# T062 — Market Sizing and Go-to-Market

**Status**: Completed
**Date**: 2026-03-12

## What was built

**Market Sizing**:

- TAM: ~280,000 Singapore SMEs with employees
- SAM: ~60,000 SMEs with active HR compliance needs (5-200 employees)
- SOM Year 1: 500 paying customers target

**Revenue Projections**:

- Year 1-3 projections with ARR growth from freemium conversion funnel

**Distribution Strategy**:

- Open platform model — no exclusive partnerships, available to all SMEs
- Association partnerships (ASME, SBF, sector trade associations) for credibility and reach

**Customer Acquisition**:

- 6 acquisition channels with estimated CAC:
  - Content marketing / SEO
  - Association partnership referrals
  - PSG listing (government marketplace)
  - LinkedIn / social media
  - HR community events and workshops
  - Referral programme

**Competitive Landscape**:

- Employment Hero — full HRIS, less SG-specific advisory depth
- Talenox — SG-focused payroll, no AI advisory
- HR consultants — high-touch but expensive, not scalable
- Generic AI (ChatGPT, etc.) — no SG regulatory specialisation or accuracy guarantees

**Pricing Strategy**:

- Freemium tier for initial adoption (limited queries, basic calculators)
- PSG subsidy calculations showing effective cost reduction for each paid tier

**Launch Plan**:

- Phase 1: Closed beta with ASME members
- Phase 2: Public launch with PSG listing
- Phase 3: Scale with association partnerships and content marketing
