# Product Brief

## Product

An AI-powered HR advisory platform for Singapore SME owners that provides comprehensive guidance across all facets of Singapore's employment laws, tripartite guidelines, and manpower policies. The platform combines advisory capabilities (legal interpretation, policy compliance, best-practice recommendations) with operational support (templates, forms, process guides) — functioning as the equivalent of having a team of top-tier HR consultants, MOM specialists, CPF advisors, union representatives, legal experts, IHRP master practitioners, TAFEP advisors, and trade association professionals on call.

## Objectives

- Provide accurate, up-to-date advisory on Singapore employment laws and regulations
- Cover all HR pillars: compensation & benefits, performance management, talent acquisition, employee relations, workforce planning, learning & development, HR operations, compliance, and more
- Account for contextual factors: sector, company size, local headcount tiers, foreign worker mix (quota/levy implications)
- Deliver both advisory (interpretation, compliance guidance) and operational support (templates, forms, step-by-step processes)
- Be accessible to SME owners who are not HR professionals
- Achieve accuracy equivalent to top-tier professional HR advisory

## Constraints

- Must reflect current Singapore law and regulations (accuracy is paramount — wrong advice has legal consequences)
- Must account for sector-specific rules (e.g., construction, marine, manufacturing have different foreign worker policies)
- Must handle the interplay between multiple regulatory bodies (MOM, CPF Board, IRAS, TAFEP, unions)
- Must distinguish between legal requirements vs. tripartite guidelines vs. best practices
- Must include appropriate disclaimers (not a substitute for legal counsel on complex matters)
- Content must be regularly updateable as laws and guidelines change

## Users

- **Primary: SME owners/directors** — Making HR decisions without dedicated HR teams, need plain-language guidance
- **Secondary: HR managers in SMEs** — Have some HR knowledge but need specialist-level advisory on complex matters
- **Tertiary: HR consultants/practitioners** — Could use as a reference tool and productivity enhancer
