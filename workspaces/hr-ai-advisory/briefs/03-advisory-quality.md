# 2 Phases
## Phase 1
1. Role-play as a team of advisors against an adversarial team of clients, asking really tough and edge questions.
    - These clients are always trying to break the rules.
    - Pay the least, pay the least benefits, work the most etc.
2. Supplement with a wide and deep research (web) on all the issues that can be used to create these scenarios.
3. The objective is to create a set of agents and skills that are of top quality advisory as envisioned in our product vision.
4. Generate a rubrics to evaluate the quality of the advisory.
5. Continuously optimize the instructions to ensure that we score well on the rubrics

# Phase 2
1. Prepare for human QA workflows
2. Think of an interface or workflow that makes it easy for humans to QA the advisory quality and give feedback.
3. Create an automated instruction finetuning so that the human QA can see improvements at the end of every session.
