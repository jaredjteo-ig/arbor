# Recruitment Module: Competitive Landscape

## Market Overview

Singapore's most popular SME HR tools (Talenox, HReasily) have **zero recruitment features**. This creates an explicit gap — every SME using these tools must bolt on a separate ATS or use spreadsheets/email/WhatsApp.

There is a pricing dead zone between "free but useless" (1-job limits on Zoho/Breezy free tiers) and "capable but expensive" ($157-299/month for real ATS features). SMEs hiring 2-5 people per year cannot justify $2,000-4,000/year for standalone ATS.

## Table Stakes (Everyone Has)

- Job posting creation and management
- Basic candidate pipeline/tracking
- Interview scheduling
- Email communication with candidates
- Some form of career page
- Basic reporting (time-to-hire, source tracking)

## Differentiators (Only Some Have)

- AI resume parsing and candidate matching (Employment Hero, Zoho, Workable, Breezy)
- AI-powered video screening (Employment Hero Recruitment Agent, Workable)
- Candidate database for proactive sourcing (Workable: 400M+ profiles)
- One-way video interviews (Workable, Breezy, Employment Hero)
- Integrated ATS + CRM (Zoho Recruit)
- Career site chatbot (Zoho Recruit Sourcing Bot)
- Multi-language career pages (Breezy: 20+, Zoho)

## Gaps Nobody Does Well

1. **MyCareersFuture integration** — no major SME platform integrates with Singapore's government job portal
2. **FCF compliance automation** — nobody tracks the 14-day posting requirement or validates salary consistency between job ad and EP application
3. **PDPA-compliant recruitment workflows** — no tool auto-manages consent, retention periods, or NRIC collection timing for candidates
4. **Employment law advisory during hiring** — no ATS tells you notice period requirements or statutory benefits obligations
5. **Integrated HRIS + recruitment for SG SMEs** — Employment Hero is the only APAC platform attempting both, but SG recruitment features lag behind AU
6. **Affordable AI for small teams** — resume parsing and matching locked behind $25-50+/user/mo tiers
7. **WhatsApp-native candidate communication** — despite WhatsApp dominance in SG, no SME ATS has native integration

## Common SME Complaints

1. Feature fragmentation — HRIS and ATS don't talk to each other
2. Candidate data scattered across email, spreadsheets, WhatsApp
3. Tools assume dedicated recruiting teams (SMEs don't have them)
4. Pricing opacity — hidden costs behind sales demos
5. AI features locked to expensive tiers
6. Onboarding disconnect — candidate-to-employee requires re-entering data
7. No compliance guidance — tools track but don't advise
8. Dashboards designed for recruiters, not business owners

## What SMEs Wish They Had

- A single system for payroll AND recruitment
- AI that actually screens candidates
- Automatic compliance with local hiring regulations (FCF, PDPA)
- WhatsApp integration for candidate communication
- Simple dashboards for business owners
- Affordable pricing that doesn't penalize small headcount
- Seamless candidate-to-employee data flow

## Arbor's Positioning Opportunity

Arbor can fill the exact gap between "Talenox/HReasily (no recruitment)" and "Workable/Breezy (expensive standalone ATS)" by offering recruitment integrated into the HRIS with Singapore-specific compliance, at a price point that makes sense for occasional hiring.

**Key differentiator**: No standalone ATS can match the hire-to-employee conversion because they always require the handoff step where data gets lost, re-keyed, or delayed. Arbor owns the full lifecycle.
