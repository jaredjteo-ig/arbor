# Market Sizing and Go-to-Market Strategy

## Market Sizing

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)

- Singapore registered businesses: ~570,000
- Businesses with employees: ~280,000
- All businesses that need HR compliance support

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)

- SMEs with 1-200 employees: ~260,000
- Active SMEs (not dormant): ~200,000
- SMEs willing to pay for HR tech: ~60,000 (30% adoption potential)

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) — Year 1-3

- Year 1 target: 500 paying SMEs (0.83% of SAM)
- Year 2 target: 2,000 paying SMEs (3.3%)
- Year 3 target: 5,000 paying SMEs (8.3%)

## Go-to-Market Strategy

### Distribution: Open Platform + Association Partnerships

**Open to all Singapore SMEs** — no restrictions on who can sign up.

**Association partnerships for distribution and credibility** (not restriction):

- ASME (Association of Small & Medium Enterprises) — distribution partner
- Singapore Business Federation — awareness channel
- SNEF (Singapore National Employers Federation) — credibility endorsement
- Industry associations (Restaurant Association, etc.) — sector-specific distribution

Partnerships provide:

1. Credibility (endorsed by trusted industry body)
2. Distribution (access to member communications)
3. Discounted pricing for members (incentive, not exclusivity)
4. Feedback loop (members as beta testers)

### Market Monitoring

- Track regional and international HRIS platforms entering or expanding in Singapore
- Monitor established SG HRIS platforms for advisory feature additions
- Watch for AI-first HR advisory startups in SG

### Pricing Strategy

**Freemium with paid tiers**:

- Free tier: 5 advisory queries/month, basic calculators
- Paid tiers: Unlimited advisory, all calculators, document generation, compliance checks
- PSG subsidy: Up to 50% off for eligible SMEs

**Effective pricing with PSG**:
| Tier | List Price | After PSG (50%) |
|------|-----------|----------------|
| Starter | $49/mo | $24.50/mo |
| Growth | $99/mo | $49.50/mo |
| Professional | $199/mo | $99.50/mo |

### Launch Plan

**Phase 1: Private Beta (Month 1-2)**

- 20-30 SMEs from ASME network
- Focus: accuracy validation, UX feedback
- Success metric: >90% accuracy, >70% satisfaction

**Phase 2: Public Beta (Month 3-4)**

- Open registration with free tier
- PSG application submitted
- Success metric: 100 sign-ups, 30 paid conversions

**Phase 3: General Availability (Month 5+)**

- Full marketing push
- PSG pre-approval expected
- Association partnership launches
- Success metric: 500 paying customers by Month 12
